# Open Questions — Richmond Quarter-Acre Garden

**Resolved:** Richmond, Kentucky / Madison County / ~2-acre yard / ~1/4-acre garden / enclosed greenhouse included.

The project no longer needs decisions about how much of the property becomes garden. That boundary is now ~10,890 sq ft.

## P0 — required to lock coordinates

1. Where should the quarter-acre garden sit relative to the house?
2. Which direction is north?
3. Where are the driveway, sheds/outbuildings, mature trees, fences, and gates?
4. Where are hose bibs / outdoor water sources?
5. Which areas are slopes, low spots, or remain wet after rain?
6. Are there septic/drain-field, utility, easement, or no-dig areas?
7. How much direct sun does the best quarter-acre candidate receive?
8. Are there existing beds/structures to preserve?

## P0 — greenhouse field check

The size is provisionally locked at **16 × 24 ft**. Remaining siting questions:
9. Which part of the quarter acre gives the greenhouse the best year-round sun?
10. Is electricity practical/safe at that location?
11. Is water practical at that location?
12. Does the site drain well?
13. What direction do major summer winds/storms commonly approach from?
14. Does a north–south long axis fit the site without creating worse shade/access problems?

These can be answered from the property map rather than from preference alone.

## P1 — major operating shapers

15. Deer pressure?
16. Rabbit/groundhog pressure?
17. Soil test available?
18. Peak-season maintenance target: roughly 3, 5, or 8+ hours/week?
19. How many people should the vegetable garden feed?
20. Fresh eating only, or meaningful freezing/canning/drying?
21. Automatic drip irrigation, manual, or hybrid?
22. Greenhouse use level: mostly seedlings/season extension, or meaningful protected vegetable production too?
23. Greenhouse winter level: unheated, frost-protection only, or actively heated?

## P2 — cultivar / use / aesthetics

24. Squash: zucchini/summer squash, winter squash, or both?
25. Daisies: Shasta daisies or another kind?
26. Geraniums: annual Pelargoniums or hardy cranesbill Geraniums?
27. Red roses: hybrid-tea form, shrub roses, climbers, or mix?
28. Keep optional one white backyard rose?
29. Wildflower tub: Kentucky-native only, or native-heavy with non-invasive annual color allowed?
30. Preferred visual direction if deviating from current recommendation of formal bones + cottage abundance?

## Best next input

A crude overhead drawing/aerial is now enough. Show:
- house
- driveway
- big trees
- fences/sheds
- water point
- north
- the general area you want the quarter-acre garden

Then the next design pass can draw the quarter-acre boundary, greenhouse, main spine, and major garden rooms before individual plant coordinates.
