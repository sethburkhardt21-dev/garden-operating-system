# Garden Vision

## Non-negotiable identity

This is a mixed edible + ornamental home garden designed to feel abundant, romantic, colorful, and intentionally maintained rather than sterile or purely utilitarian.

Core aesthetic:
- vigorous productive vegetables
- classic red roses as deliberate focal points
- one optional white rose in the backyard only
- cut-flower abundance
- pollinator-friendly perennial structure
- vertical vines used architecturally
- a separate deliberately chaotic-looking wildflower tub
- clean paths, repeated materials, and consistent supports so the abundance reads as intentional

## Three layers of success

### 1. Biological success
Plants receive the right light, moisture, soil, spacing, airflow, seasonal timing, and pest/disease management.

### 2. Human success
The garden can be maintained in short sessions without needing to remember everything.

### 3. Style success
The space has visual rhythm: repetition, height gradients, focal points, coordinated supports, deliberate edges, and continuous bloom rather than “plants placed wherever they fit.”
