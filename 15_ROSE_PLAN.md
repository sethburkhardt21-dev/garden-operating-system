# Rose Plan

## Design invariant
Primary rose color = classic red.
Optional exception = one white rose in the backyard.

## Kentucky-specific provisional risk
Black spot deserves first-class design treatment rather than being handled only after symptoms appear.

## Selection criteria
1. true red color and desired classic flower form
2. disease resistance under humid conditions
3. repeat bloom
4. mature size appropriate to location
5. winter hardiness for exact site
6. fragrance (if desired)
7. availability
8. maintenance burden

## Placement rules
- strong sun
- free air movement
- irrigation at soil level
- adequate spacing at mature width
- easy deadhead/prune access
- no dense wet hedge directly crowding the canes

## Do not purchase yet
until exact rose location, mature-size budget, and sun map are known.
