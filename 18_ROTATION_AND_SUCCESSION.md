# Rotation + Succession

## Disease-family ledger

### Solanaceae
tomatoes; jalapeño; bell pepper; eggplant; both potato groups

### Cucurbitaceae
English cucumber; squash; pumpkin

### Brassicaceae
cabbage

### Apiaceae
parsley; cilantro

### Lamiaceae
basil; rosemary; thyme; lavender

Rotation should be recorded by family, not merely by crop name.

## Succession candidates
- cilantro: frequent short intervals during suitable cool periods
- basil: repeated sowings/plantings can maintain tender production
- cucumber: additional planting may extend harvest where season allows
- summer squash: later plantings may replace declining plants
- tomatoes/peppers: staggered plantings can extend production where space and transplant supply permit
- flowers such as zinnia/cosmos: repeat sowing can maintain peak bloom

## Small-garden nuance
Perfect multi-year rotation may be impossible. If so, reduce risk using:
- clean plant material
- resistant cultivars
- sanitation
- airflow
- soil health
- containers/new media for selected crops
- precise disease identification
- rotation where it actually fits
