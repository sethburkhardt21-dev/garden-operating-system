# Visual Masterplan + Bed Grid — v0.6

**Status:** CANONICAL DEFAULT GEOMETRY / REAL-PARCEL PLACEMENT STILL PROVISIONAL  
**Location:** Richmond, Kentucky  
**Canvas:** 90 ft east–west × 121 ft south–north = 10,890 sq ft

This file turns the v0.5 room plan into a bed-level default map. It is still a normalized design, not a survey of the real property. The entire plan may later be rotated, mirrored, or translated without changing its internal logic.

## Important v0.6 reconciliation

The v0.5 documents described four approximately 12 × 24 ft rotation blocks but the single `ROTATION-NE` envelope was not large enough to contain all four. v0.6 resolves that inconsistency.

The canonical annual rotation field is now the combined east-middle area:
- `x=47–86`
- `y=37–77`

Inside it are four **16 × 16 ft** rotation blocks, each 256 sq ft, separated by service paths. Total active rotation soil = **1,024 sq ft**. This is ample for the normal first-year crop quantities and is easier to maintain than four oversized 12 × 24 ft blocks.
