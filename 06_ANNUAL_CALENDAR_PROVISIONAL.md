# Annual Calendar — Provisional Northern Kentucky Version

**Do not convert to exact calendar dates until ZIP is confirmed.**

## September — current planning window
- inventory remaining summer crops and flowers
- photograph beds before cleanup so next year's layout history exists
- order garlic planting stock now if not already purchased
- identify a sunny, well-drained garlic bed that will stay occupied until early summer
- choose where permanent asparagus and rhubarb beds will go next spring
- mark which dahlias are worth saving
- start measuring sun/bed geometry while foliage is still present
- soil-test areas intended for major 2027 planting before adding random amendments
- document pest/disease symptoms now rather than throwing all evidence away at cleanup

## October–early November
- plant garlic when site and soil conditions fit local Extension guidance
- mulch garlic after planting
- after frost, manage spent warm-season crops and diseased residue appropriately
- lift and prepare dahlia tubers for storage after top growth is frost-killed/matured as appropriate
- clean supports, cages, trellises
- record 2026 failures before memory fades

## Winter
- select exact cultivars
- design the scaled map
- order seed
- finalize trellises/supports
- build seed-start calendar backwards from local planting window
- choose rose cultivars with disease resistance as a major criterion
- select wildflower mix by region, not generic “wildflower” label

## Late winter / early spring
- seed-start warm-season plants according to cultivar and local frost window
- prepare permanent asparagus/rhubarb areas
- establish cool-season flowers/crops on their own calendar
- inspect irrigation before plants need it

## Frost-safe warm season
- harden off tomatoes, peppers, eggplant
- transplant based on soil/weather, not a single magic date
- direct sow/transplant cucurbits when warm enough
- install supports at planting, not after vines collapse

## Summer
- water by soil need + weather
- maintain mulch
- train vines and tomatoes
- scout weekly for disease/pests
- deadhead/cut flowers
- harvest frequently
- succession sow basil/cilantro where useful
- photograph recurring issues with date + bed
