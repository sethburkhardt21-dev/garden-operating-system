# Cultivar Matrix — Richmond, Kentucky Pass v0.6

**Status:** LOCATION + GARDEN SCALE CONFIRMED / CULTIVARS PROVISIONAL until availability, exact bed conditions, disease-resistance evidence, and user taste/use preferences are checked.

This file resolves broad requested categories into serious candidates without silently replacing the original request. A cultivar is not `LOCKED` until site fit, use, disease profile, and realistic availability are checked.

## Evidence rule

Primary Kentucky sources:
- University of Kentucky Extension `ID-128` — Home Vegetable Gardening in Kentucky
- University of Kentucky Extension `ID-133` — Vegetable Cultivars for Kentucky Gardens
- University of Kentucky 2026 disease-resistance guidance
- current UK Crop Diversification resources
- Madison County Extension for local questions/diagnostics
- historical UK rose-resistance research only as age-caveated candidate evidence

Because some cultivar tables are older than the current season, newer disease-resistance information and current availability receive extra weight.

## Site effect of the quarter-acre design

The Garden OS has enough room to avoid false scarcity, but it is intentionally **not** maximum production.

Strategy:
- pair flavor-first and disease-first tomatoes without overplanting
- use normal household quantities
- choose pumpkins/squash by use and vine behavior, not by available 2-acre yard space
- choose roses by mature width and disease pressure, not by how many can physically fit
- reserve room for airflow, succession, flowers, and access

# Tomatoes

## Heirloom tomato
**Leading candidate: Cherokee Purple**
- appears in Kentucky-recommended cultivar resources
- strength: classic heirloom flavor/appearance
- weakness: less disease insurance than many modern hybrids
- role: flavor/showpiece tomato rather than the only tomato carrying harvest

**2027 default:** 2 plants in K01.

## Beefsteak tomato
**Leading candidate: Big Beef**
- Kentucky cultivar guidance lists Big Beef with multiple resistance traits
- strong fit for large slicer/beefsteak role
- indeterminate habit requires strong support

**Alternative: classic Beefsteak**
- preserves literal traditional type
- use only if source/disease profile are acceptable

**2027 default:** 2 plants in K02.

## Cherry tomato
**Tier 1 candidates:**
1. `Sun Gold` — strong flavor reputation and Kentucky disease-resilience history; orange fruit.
2. `Mountain Magic` — resilience-forward option.
3. `Matt's Wild Cherry` — disease-resilient small-fruited option; extremely vigorous.

**2027 default:** 2 plants in K03, potentially one flavor-first + one resilience-first if the user accepts different cultivars/colors.

# Herbs

## Basil
- Genovese-type for primary culinary use
- disease-resistant basil if downy mildew pressure warrants it

## Rosemary
Select a cold-tolerant cultivar but do not assume winter survival. One containerized plant remains sensible insurance until actual microclimate performance is observed.

## Thyme
English/common culinary thyme is the default candidate. Creeping thyme is ornamental, not a substitute for kitchen thyme.

## Parsley
Flat-leaf Italian is the leading culinary candidate unless curly form is preferred aesthetically.

## Cilantro
Favor slow-bolting types because Richmond heat shortens the leaf period.

## Lavender
Choose cold-hardy English lavender (`Lavandula angustifolia` type) and use P03's independent dry/drainage zone.

# Roses

Rose cultivar selection must satisfy two priorities simultaneously:
1. **classic red visual character**
2. strong enough disease performance for humid Kentucky conditions

University of Kentucky identifies black spot as a major Kentucky rose problem. Older UK/multi-state resistance compilations provide useful historical candidates, but they are not current guarantees.

## Classic red historical candidates for current re-check
- `Mister Lincoln`
- `Olympiad`
- `Alec's Red`
- `Chrysler Imperial`

**Current first-check pair:** `Mister Lincoln` and `Olympiad` because they preserve the classic high-centered red role.

## Lower-maintenance red shrub backup option
Historical regional research identified:
- `Home Run`
- `Knock Out`

These may serve as a disease-resilient structural/focal option if the user accepts a shrub/landscape form for one or more of the 3–5 red roses.

## Optional white rose
Older resistance lists include `Polarstern` as a candidate to investigate, but the project allows only 0–1 white rose and only in the backyard/interior garden.

See `46_ROSE_AND_FLOWER_DESIGN.md`.

# Cultivar locking protocol

A cultivar becomes `LOCKED` only after:
1. requested category preserved
2. Richmond/site fit checked
3. disease-resistance evidence checked
4. mature size fits its exact v0.6 bed/position
5. user taste/color/use preference checked
6. seed/plant source realistically available
7. final choice recorded in `04_PLANT_MASTER_LEDGER.csv`
8. final choice recorded in `29_DECISION_LOG.md`
