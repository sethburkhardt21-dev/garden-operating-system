## Climate baseline

Richmond/Madison County sits around the USDA 2023 **6b–7a transition** at coarse city/ZIP scale. Until the exact parcel is pinned:
- prefer permanent plants hardy to 6b where practical
- treat hardiness zone as winter-survival guidance, not a planting calendar
- use Kentucky Extension timing + actual weather/frost/soil conditions for annual crops

Richmond garden implications:
- four-season climate
- hot, humid summers
- significant fungal-disease pressure on tomatoes, cucurbits, and roses
- variable frost behavior across slopes/low spots
- garlic fits fall planting
- asparagus and rhubarb need permanent beds
- dahlias need winter-storage planning
- greenhouse summer ventilation is as important as winter heat

- sit within the quarter-acre garden, preferably near the entrance/work hub

- have practical water access
