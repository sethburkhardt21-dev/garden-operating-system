# Weather Playbooks — Skeleton

## Frost warning
BEFORE: harvest tender fruit; move movable containers; cover tender crops with supported frost protection; secure covers.
DURING: avoid crushing foliage with heavy wet material.
AFTER: uncover when safe; inspect tissue; do not immediately prune every mildly damaged leaf before extent is clear.

## Heat wave
BEFORE: deep-water based on soil need; check mulch; shade new transplants/containers if necessary.
DURING: prioritize root-zone moisture; avoid high-stress transplanting/pruning; monitor containers.
AFTER: inspect blossom/fruit drop and sunscald; resume normal routine gradually.

## High wind / storm
BEFORE: tie tomatoes; secure trellises; move containers; pick nearly ripe heavy fruit where appropriate.
AFTER: reset roots if lifted; support split stems; photograph damage; remove only irreparable tissue.

## Extreme rain
BEFORE: stop scheduled irrigation.
AFTER: inspect drainage and root-zone saturation; do not walk/compact very wet bed soil; scout for foliar disease in following days.

## Vacation
A separate checklist will be generated after irrigation system + trip length are known.
