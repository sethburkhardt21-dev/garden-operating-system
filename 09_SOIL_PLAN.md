# Soil Plan

## First principle
Test before aggressively amending.

## Separate requirements
The garden should not be treated as one uniform soil environment.

### Rich, moisture-retentive but drained production soil
Most annual vegetables and cut flowers.

### Excellent-drainage zone
Lavender, rosemary, thyme.

### Permanent perennial edible soil
Asparagus and rhubarb: prepare deeply before planting because future correction is harder.

### Rose zone
Good drainage + organic matter + airflow/splash management.

## Before adding fertilizer
Obtain a soil test for:
- pH
- phosphorus
- potassium
- organic matter if offered
- fertilizer recommendations

## Avoid
- automatic lime without pH result
- repeated high-phosphorus amendments “just in case”
- burying unfinished material in planting holes
- raw manure immediately around active food crops
- creating a wet rich soil for lavender simply because vegetables like it
